Discussion Items

Time Item Who Notes
2000 Meeting setup and roll call JA
  • Meeting opened with roll call.
  • Recording and transcription confirmed.
  • No patents declared.
2010 Building Blocks status and CRS ontology TH
  • TH reported progress on publishing CRS ontology Building Blocks.
  • Discussed challenges publishing Building Blocks alongside existing GitHub Pages content.
  • Shared links to deployed Building Blocks for review.
2025 Granularity of Building Blocks documentation TH, NC
  • Discussion on whether Building Blocks should document individual classes/functions or reusable groupings.
  • Consensus that Building Blocks should align with reusable “chunks” rather than every class individually.
2040 GeoSPARQL 3D and IFC use cases TS
  • TS presented background on IFC geometry, 3D complexity, and challenges aligning IFC with GeoSPARQL.
  • Outlined a draft questionnaire intended to capture IFC community perspectives on GeoSPARQL 3D scope.
  • Emphasised the need to distinguish what is actually used vs theoretically possible.
2100 GeoSPARQL–IFC alignment discussion NC, JA, PC, TH
  • Discussion focused on GeoSPARQL providing fundamental 3D geometry and topological relations rather than deep IFC semantics.
  • Examples raised from hydrology and building models to illustrate lightweight alignment benefits.
  • Agreement that tolerances and geometric fuzziness are challenging and may not belong in core GeoSPARQL.
2140 Questionnaire refinement and distribution All
  • Agreement to refine questionnaire to capture current usage and barriers to adoption.
  • Target communities identified: GeoSemantics DWG, implementers group, and Linked Building Data community (via Arcadios).
2155 Close All MEETING ENDS
